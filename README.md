# 🎓 Free Online Certificate Courses — All Domains

A student-friendly, community-maintained directory of online courses, learning paths, certificates, and digital badges across technical and non-technical fields.

**Goal:** help students discover useful learning opportunities and understand whether the learning content, certificate, badge, or assessment is free.

> **Important:** “Free course” does not automatically mean “free certificate.” Providers can change their policies, eligibility rules, regional availability, and assessment fees. Always check the official course page before enrolling or paying.

## Certificate status key

- **Free certificate / credential:** provider advertises a free certificate or credential for the specified learning activity. Check course-level eligibility.
- **Free badge:** a digital badge or learning achievement is available; it may not be an accredited certificate.
- **Free learning; paid certificate/exam:** course materials may be free, but a certificate, proctored assessment, or certification exam may cost money.
- **Varies:** pricing and certificate availability depend on the specific course, promotion, location, or financial-aid approval.

## How to use this directory

1. Choose a domain that matches your learning goal.
2. Open the provider's official website.
3. Check prerequisites, language, duration, and current fees.
4. Confirm certificate conditions before starting.
5. Save your certificate or badge link in your portfolio when appropriate.
