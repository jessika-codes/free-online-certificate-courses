# Healthcare & Life Sciences

Provider and course-directory links are starting points. Check the exact course page for current availability, fees, prerequisites, and credential conditions.

| Provider / resource | Official link | Learning and credential notes |
|---|---|---|
| OpenWHO | [https://openwho.org/](https://openwho.org/) | Free public-health learning; certificates are available for selected courses subject to course rules. |
| WHO Academy | [https://whoacademy.org/](https://whoacademy.org/) | Health learning resources; credential availability varies by course. |

| Alison | [https://alison.com/](https://alison.com/) | Free health and safety course learning; certificates generally paid. |

| CDC TRAIN | [https://www.train.org/cdctrain/](https://www.train.org/cdctrain/) | Public-health training catalog; credit/certificate availability depends on course. |

## Before enrolling

- Confirm the course itself is currently available.
- Check whether the certificate, badge, assessment, or exam is free.
- Check regional eligibility, deadlines, language, and prerequisites.
- Record the date you verified the details when contributing an update.
