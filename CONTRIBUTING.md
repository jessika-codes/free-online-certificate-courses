# Contributing

Thank you for helping learners find trustworthy educational resources!

## Add a course or provider

Please submit only official provider/course links. Do not add affiliate or referral links.

For each entry, include:
- Provider and exact course name (when known)
- Official URL
- Domain and difficulty level
- Whether course access is free
- Whether the certificate, badge, or exam is free
- Conditions (audit-only, financial aid, regional restrictions, deadlines, paid exam, etc.)
- Date last verified (`YYYY-MM-DD`)

## Suggested format

| Course | Provider | Official link | Level | Course fee | Credential fee | Last checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example course | Example provider | https://example.org/course | Beginner | Free | Verify on course page | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Quality rules

1. Never describe a certificate as free unless the official provider confirms it.
2. Distinguish certificates, digital badges, statements of participation, and professional certification exams.
3. Do not promise accreditation, employment, or universal employer recognition.
4. Prefer current, direct links over third-party summaries.
5. Be respectful and keep descriptions concise.
