# Certificate guide

## Credential types

- **Certificate of completion:** confirms that a learner completed a course or its requirements.
- **Digital badge:** a shareable digital record of a skill or achievement; it may include metadata about issuer and criteria.
- **Statement of participation:** confirms participation or engagement, and may not represent a graded qualification.
- **Professional certification:** usually involves a defined assessment or exam and may have fees.
- **Academic credit:** formal credit awarded by an eligible educational institution; do not assume a free online course provides it.

## Questions to check before enrolling

1. Is all course content free, or only an audit preview?
2. Is the certificate free, or is there a separate fee?
3. Is a proctored exam required?
4. Does financial aid apply to the certificate fee?
5. Are there regional restrictions or time-limited promotions?
6. Who issues the credential?
7. Does the credential have assessment criteria?

## Safe wording for listings

Use “Free learning; certificate fee applies” when course content is free but a credential costs money. Use “Certificate conditions vary” when you cannot confirm the current policy. Never infer certificate eligibility from a course being free to audit.
