# Mathematics, Science & Engineering

Provider and course-directory links are starting points. Check the exact course page for current availability, fees, prerequisites, and credential conditions.

| Provider / resource | Official link | Learning and credential notes |
|---|---|---|
| NPTEL | [https://nptel.ac.in/](https://nptel.ac.in/) | Free course learning across engineering and science; certificate exam is paid. |
| SWAYAM | [https://swayam.gov.in/](https://swayam.gov.in/) | Free university-level courses; certificate exam generally has a fee. |
| Khan Academy | [https://www.khanacademy.org/](https://www.khanacademy.org/) | Free mathematics, physics, chemistry, and science learning; no general course certificate. |
| MIT OpenCourseWare | [https://ocw.mit.edu/](https://ocw.mit.edu/) | Free university course materials; generally no certificate. |
| OpenLearn | [https://www.open.edu/openlearn/](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/) | Free science and mathematics courses with selected statements/badges. |
| Saylor Academy | [https://www.saylor.org/](https://www.saylor.org/) | Free courses in mathematics and science with eligible completion certificates. |

## Before enrolling

- Confirm the course itself is currently available.
- Check whether the certificate, badge, assessment, or exam is free.
- Check regional eligibility, deadlines, language, and prerequisites.
- Record the date you verified the details when contributing an update.
