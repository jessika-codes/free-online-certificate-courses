# Law & Legal Studies

Provider and course-directory links are starting points. Check the exact course page for current availability, fees, prerequisites, and credential conditions.

| Provider / resource | Official link | Learning and credential notes |
|---|---|---|
| SWAYAM | [https://swayam.gov.in/](https://swayam.gov.in/) | Free access to selected law and legal-studies courses; certification exam generally paid. |
| OpenLearn | [https://www.open.edu/openlearn/](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/) | Free law, society, and rights-related courses with selected statements/badges. |
| Alison | [https://alison.com/](https://alison.com/) | Free legal-studies course learning; certificates generally paid. |
| edX | [https://www.edx.org/learn/law](https://www.edx.org/learn/law) | Law courses can often be audited free; verified certificates generally paid. |
| Coursera | [https://www.coursera.org/browse/social-sciences/law](https://www.coursera.org/browse/social-sciences/law) | Some course content may be accessible free; certificate terms vary. |

## Before enrolling

- Confirm the course itself is currently available.
- Check whether the certificate, badge, assessment, or exam is free.
- Check regional eligibility, deadlines, language, and prerequisites.
- Record the date you verified the details when contributing an update.
