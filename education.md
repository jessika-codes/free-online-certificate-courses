Provider and course-directory links are starting points. Check the exact course page for current availability, fees, prerequisites, and credential conditions.

## Before enrolling

- Confirm the course itself is currently available.
- Check whether the certificate, badge, assessment, or exam is free.
- Check regional eligibility, deadlines, language, and prerequisites.
- Record the date you verified the details when contributing an update.
